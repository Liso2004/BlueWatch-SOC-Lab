How to use this tool
  1. Pick a test app from the list (real apps from a research dataset that the checker never saw while learning), or paste an app’s details under Check another app.
  2. Read the result: “Needs a look” or “No alert”, the risk score, and why.
  3. Press Write AI case brief for a plain-English summary you can paste into a ticket.
  4. After checking, record your decision. If it needs follow-up, press Open an incident.

How to use this

Pick the Android version on a customer’s phone to see known security flaws for it, from the US government’s National Vulnerability Database. This tells you how exposed the phone is. It does not change an app’s risk result, and “no flaws found” does not mean an app is safe.
