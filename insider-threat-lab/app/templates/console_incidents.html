Incidents

Problems BlueWatch found that a person needs to look at.

How to use this page
  1. Pick an incident from the list — newest and most serious are at the top.
  2. Assign it to the person who will handle it, and set it to Investigating.
  3. Tag it so the team can find similar cases (e.g. credential-attack, insider-threat).
  4. Write what you checked in Notes. When done, mark it Resolved — or False alarm if nothing was wrong.

Every change is saved straight away and added to the incident’s timeline.
