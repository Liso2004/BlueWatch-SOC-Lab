Overview

BlueWatch watches three threats to SecureBank customers: password guessing, insiders copying customer data, and Android banking malware on customers’ phones. Why this matters

Three in four South African smartphones run Android (StatCounter, June 2026), and Kaspersky counted 162,275 new mobile banking trojan packages in the first three months of 2026 alone. These trojans draw fake screens over banking apps, read SMS codes and tap for the user. Staff accounts are targets too: a guessed password or an insider can expose thousands of customer records.
